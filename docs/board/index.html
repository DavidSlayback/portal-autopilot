Portal Autopilot

Every “log in to view” email, handled by two Grok Bots.

60-second talk track

Your hospital, your insurer and your bank never email you the message. They email “log in to view.” Every assistant stops right there, and “you have a secure message” is one of the most common phishing lures.

Sentry checks every notification against portals you registered: sender, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, look-alike domains. It never opens the emailed link. Clerk logs into the real portal at its registered address, files the document, and puts the deadline on your calendar.

Then it compares the hospital bill with the insurer’s EOB, claim by claim, in code. Here that found $372 you don’t owe and a $412 autopay about to go out. Clerk drafts the dispute and sends it only after you approve.

Your inbox says “log in to view.” Portal Autopilot logs in, reads it, and catches what you don’t owe.

Security model

  • never opens a link from an email
  • only opens portal addresses you registered
  • you sign in once yourself; the Bot never sees the password
  • money math is code, not the model
  • nothing is sent or changed without your OK
  • text inside portals is data, never instructions
